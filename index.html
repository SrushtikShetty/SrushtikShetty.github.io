About

Curious enough to dig past the surface.

I like the uncomfortable part of a problem — the point where a system stops behaving cleanly, where the wrong assumptions become obvious, and where the only path forward is to understand the mechanism itself.

My work sits between machine learning, systems engineering, and experimentation. I’m interested in how data drives decisions, how infrastructure behaves under stress, and how small choices can scale into meaningful outcomes.

Outside the screen, I value strategy, pattern recognition, and the discipline of reducing a problem to its essential mechanics before building the solution.

The goal is not to chase trends. It is to build reliable understanding, ask better questions, and keep learning with intention.
